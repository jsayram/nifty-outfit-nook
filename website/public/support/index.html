Get help

Write to [support email]. Tell us what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. Please don't send your measurements, photos of yourself or details of your closet. We don't need them to help.

In the app, Send feedback (in Profile, or the middle desk drawer on Home) shows exactly what will be sent before you send it. Add a reply address if you'd like an answer: a note without one can't be answered. We usually reply within [reply time].

Report a problem with a result

If the stylist says something wrong or hurtful, or a try-on picture comes out wrong, use Report a problem on that result. We read every report. If you include a try-on picture, we get the version without your face.

Your subscription and credits

  • Cancel or change: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or in the app: Profile, Styling access, Manage subscription.
  • Refunds: Apple handles them, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • New phone, or reinstalled the app: Profile, Styling access, Restore purchases.
  • Free unlock code: Profile, Styling access, Redeem a code. It doesn't cancel a subscription you already have.
  • Picture credits don't expire, and they still work after styling access ends. They're given up if you delete your account.
  • Deleting the app or your data doesn't cancel a subscription.

Your data and privacy

  • Delete everything: Profile, Privacy and terms, Delete my data. It removes everything from this iPhone and your iCloud copy. Delete my account also removes what our server holds.
  • Turn a feature off: Profile, Privacy and terms. Each switch stops future sending for that feature.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of what we hold: write to [support email] with the subject "Privacy request".
  • Full details are in the privacy policy.

Common questions

Does it work offline?

Your closet, looks, suitcases and making your own looks work offline. Style Me, swaps, Ask stylist, live weather and try-on pictures need a connection.

Is my closet backed up?

Only if you turn on iCloud sync, or export a backup in Profile. Deleting the app deletes the copy on that iPhone.

Why doesn't a try-on picture fit like the real thing?

Try-on pictures are simulations made by AI. They show the look, not the real fit.

Where did my face go in "See what was sent"?

That's on purpose. Your face never leaves your phone: the app covers it before sending and puts it back afterwards.

Security

Found a security problem? Write to [support email] with the subject "Security", or use private vulnerability reporting on the app's GitHub repository. Please don't post it publicly.
