Who can use it

You need to be 18 or older. You can use your closet without an account. Online features need you to sign in with Apple and confirm you're 18 or older.

AI suggestions come with no guarantee

Outfit ideas, swaps, stylist answers and try-on pictures are made by AI. They can be wrong, odd or simply not to your taste. A try-on picture is a simulation: it shows the look, not how a piece will really fit, and it isn't a photo of you wearing it. Fit notes are guidance, not a sizing promise. Nothing in the app is medical, health or weight advice. If something comes out wrong or hurtful, use Report a problem on it.

What's free and what's paid

Your closet, looks, suitcases, laundry, making your own looks, search, iCloud sync and backups stay free, even if you stop paying. Styling access pays for the AI stylist and its monthly try-on pictures, within the limits the app shows. Limits keep the service affordable, and we may pause an AI feature for everyone if a provider fails or costs spike. The app will say so, and your closet keeps working.

Subscriptions, credits and refunds

  • Styling access renews automatically until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings. Apple bills you. Deleting the app or your data doesn't cancel it.
  • Picture credits pay for extra try-on pictures. They don't expire, and they still work after styling access ends. A picture that couldn't be made doesn't use a credit. One that was made does, even if you don't like it, and even if your connection dropped before it reached your phone. Credits have no cash value and can't be moved to another account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, under Apple's rules, at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunds credits you already used, we don't take anything back from your next pack.
  • Free unlock codes work once and can't be sold. Redeeming one doesn't cancel a subscription you already have.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time in Profile, Privacy and terms, Delete my account. It removes what our server holds and everything on that iPhone. Unused picture credits are given up when you delete your account, and the screen shows how many you have before you confirm. Signing in again later makes a new, empty account. Deleting doesn't cancel a subscription, so cancel that in your Apple Account first.

Your content, and fair use

Your photos, notes and looks are yours. You let us use them only to run the features you turn on, as the privacy policy describes. Use photos of yourself and of clothes you're allowed to photograph. Don't upload photos of other people, of anyone under 18, or nude or sexual images. Don't try to get around limits or use the app to break the law. We may close an account that breaks these terms.

Changes

We may change or stop features. If we stop the app, we'll say so in the app where we can, and you can still export your closet. When these terms change, the date at the top changes.
