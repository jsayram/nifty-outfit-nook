Privacy policy

Draft of 29 October 2026

Draft, not published. The contact address and publisher's name are placeholders.

The short version

  • Your closet, photos, measurements and looks stay on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. We don't keep a copy of your closet.
  • Nothing leaves your phone for a feature until you turn that feature on. Each feature asks on its own.
  • When a feature does send something, this page names who gets it, what they get and how long they keep it.
  • Your face never leaves your phone, except into your own iCloud and your own backups.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

Nifty Outfit Nook is an iPhone and iPad app that helps you put outfits together from clothes you already own. It's made and published by [publisher's legal name] ("we"). Questions about privacy go to [support email].

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you add: your pieces and their photos, suitcases, laundry marks, looks, try-on pictures, your try-on photo, the name on your sign, your fit, measurements and weight, and your preferences. The app protects these files with your iPhone's strongest file protection, and hides your measurements and pictures in the app switcher.

iCloud sync is off until you turn it on. Then a copy of everything, your measurements, weight and try-on photo included, goes to your private iCloud, which only your Apple Account can reach. We can't read it. Apple holds the keys to most iCloud data unless you turn on Advanced Data Protection in Settings. With it on, everything the app puts in iCloud is end-to-end encrypted, apart from the names of record types and their dates and sizes.

Backups you export are encrypted with a passphrase. The file goes only where you save it.

Features that send data, only when you allow them

Your account

Online features need you to sign in with Apple and confirm you're 18 or older. Apple gives our server a private ID for you. We ask Apple for no name and no email. Our server keeps that ID, your consent choices, your usage limits and your purchases. You don't need an account to use your closet.

The app also uses Apple's App Attest to check that requests come from the real app. That keeps a key for your app install on our server.

Style Me, swaps and Ask stylist

When you tap Style me, swap a piece or ask the stylist, the app sends Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, what that request needs: short descriptions of the pieces involved (kind, colour, the names you gave them), the occasion, the weather conditions (like "cool and rainy", never your location), any note you type, and your recent looks so ideas don't repeat. Your height, measurements and weight may go too when they help with the request, like your inseam for trousers. It never gets your photos, your face, your name, your account ID or your location.

Anthropic doesn't use this to train its models, and deletes it within 30 days. If its safety checks flag a request, it can keep it for up to 2 years. Requests may be processed outside the US.

Weather

If you turn on live weather, the app asks Apple Weather for the forecast using a city you pick or, if you allow it, a rough location. Only the weather conditions go into Style Me. We don't keep your location.

Try-on pictures

Before anything is sent, your iPhone covers your head and hair with flat grey and replaces the room behind you with a plain studio backdrop. If it can't find your face or can't confirm the cover, nothing is sent.

A photo of your body, without your face and on a plain backdrop, and your clothing photos go to Black Forest Labs, who may keep them and use them to improve their models. On the way, our server holds them, encrypted, for up to 30 minutes while your picture is made, then deletes them. We can't delete copies Black Forest Labs keeps, and deleting your data in the app doesn't remove them.

Black Forest Labs sends back the dressed picture, and your iPhone puts your own face back in. The finished picture is saved on your iPhone, and in your iCloud if sync is on. Every try-on picture has "See what was sent", which shows exactly what left your phone. No measurements, notes or names go with try-on.

Feedback, support and reports

If you send feedback, we get the words you saw before sending, and only what you chose to add: a reply address, the app and iOS version and your iPhone model, three closet counts, and one screenshot with its location and other photo details removed.

If you report something the stylist or try-on made, we get your reason, any note you add, which result it was and when it was made. We get the picture only if you choose to include it, and then only the version without your face.

We delete notes and reports 90 days after we close them, or 180 days after they arrive if we never do, and with your account. Crash and speed details go along only on a note where you turn them on: counts and timings, never your content.

Purchases

Apple handles payment, so we never see your card. We get a record of what you bought (styling access, a free unlock or picture credits) so the app knows what you have.

Help improve the app

Off unless you turn it on in Privacy and terms. If it's on, the app sends simple events, like "Style Me finished", with the app version, iPhone or iPad, and a random ID you can reset. They're sent without your account, so we can't link them to you. Never what you type, your photos, measurements, closet, looks or location. Raw events are deleted after 30 days, and only totals are kept. Turning it off clears anything not yet sent, and "Delete my analytics data" removes what we hold under your current ID.

How long we keep things

  • Your closet, photos, looks and measurements: on your devices and your iCloud, until you delete them. Recently deleted pieces: 30 days.
  • Styling answers: up to 24 hours on our server, so a dropped connection doesn't cost you another request.
  • Support notes and reports: 90 days after we close them, or 180 days if we never do.
  • Usage limits and the credit record stay for their usage window, so deleting and starting again doesn't reset them.
  • Security logs hold no personal content and are kept 30 days. Server backups are encrypted and kept 30 days.

Your choices, and deleting your data

  • Turn a feature off in Profile, Privacy and terms. It stops future sending at once.
  • Delete my data removes everything from this iPhone and your iCloud copy.
  • Delete my account also removes what our server holds and stops Sign in with Apple for this app. Unused picture credits are given up, and the screen shows how many first. It doesn't cancel a subscription; manage that in your Apple Account.
  • What we can't delete: requests Anthropic still holds for up to 30 days (longer if flagged), photos Black Forest Labs keeps, and backups until they expire within 30 days. Deleted accounts don't come back from a backup.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold, a correction or deletion at [support email], with the subject "Privacy request".

What we never do

No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. No selling or renting your data, and no data brokers. We don't use your photos or measurements to score your body or to train our own models.

Age

Nifty Outfit Nook is for adults. Online features ask you to confirm you're 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you think a child has sent us something, write to [support email] and we'll delete it.

Security

Your data is encrypted on your device and on the way. Apart from the try-on photos held for up to 30 minutes, our server keeps no photos and no copy of your closet. Keys to the AI services live only on our server, never in the app.

This website

This website has no analytics, cookies, forms or trackers. Our host, Cloudflare, sees the usual request details, like your IP address, to serve the page. Because nothing here tracks you, there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to turn off.

Changes and contact

When this page changes, the date at the top changes. If a change means a feature sends something new, or sends to a new company, the app asks you again first. Contact: [support email].
