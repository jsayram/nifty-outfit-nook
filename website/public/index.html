Your clothes.
Working together.

Nifty Outfit Nook is being built to remember what you own, help style it, picture new looks, pack for trips, and keep laundry in the plan.

Closet

Everything you own, easy to find.

Search and filter by type, colour, suitcase, and laundry status. Open any piece to style with it, edit it, or pack it.

AI styling assistant

Outfit help that starts with your wardrobe.

Choose an occasion, weather, comfort, colours, and a closet or suitcase. The assistant is being built to create outfit ideas from the pieces you already own.

01

Start with what is available

Style from your whole closet or only the pieces packed in a suitcase, while keeping laundry status in view.

Tell it what today needs

Add the occasion, weather, comfort, colour, and a preference for this request without changing your whole profile.

03

Keep shaping the look

Save an outfit, swap one piece, ask the stylist about it, or choose Try It On when you want a picture.

Current announcements.

Follow the nook
as it takes shape.

From the first plan in September to the latest development milestones. Follow the app as it grows into a complete iPhone and iPad experience.

Dates mark the milestones. Screen previews show the current development UI with sample data; the early mockup is labelled separately.

  1. ✓

    September 26, 2026Starting point

    The product plan

    The first product plan set the purpose: organise the clothes you own, build useful outfits, save looks, and make personal styling feel easier.

    Planned
  2. ✓

    October 3–13, 2026Shaped together

    Reviewed with Lily

    Lily’s feedback shaped the everyday experience. Review rounds settled the warm neutral rooms, personal preferences, one outfit in three versions, and easier ways to change a piece.

    Reviewed
  3. ✓

    October 13, 2026A name and a first hello

    Meet Nifty Outfit Nook

    The app received its name, the native build began, and this coming-soon website opened its doors.

    Milestone complete
  4. ✓

    October 14, 2026The foundation

    The SwiftUI pieces

    Colours, typography, controls, illustrations, and reusable components came together as the app’s design system, with light, dark, and large-text checks.

    Built
  5. ✓

    October 14–16, 2026The app takes shape

    The rooms became a journey

    Home, Closet, outfit ideas, saved looks, Suitcases, Laundry, and Profile became native screens. Settings, iPad layouts, short tours, and onboarding connected the sample-data experience.

    Built with sample data
  6. ✓

    October 17, 2026A more realistic closet

    Real sample photos across the app

    Garment photos, cutouts, and example try-on previews replaced stand-ins throughout the sample wardrobe. Accessibility and screen-size checks helped refine the experience.

    Sample experience checked
  7. ✓

    October 20, 2026Keeping your wardrobe

    Local saving and backup recovery

    Wardrobe records and drafts can now survive closing and reopening the development app. Encrypted, passphrase-protected exports and restore flows are built and tested. Optional iCloud sync is still to come.

    Built and tested
  8. ✓

    October 20–21, 2026Adding your own pieces

    Photos, paste, and image storage

    The development build can import clothing photos from the camera, photo library, or a copied image. Kept copies have identifying metadata removed, with thumbnails and storage limits to keep browsing manageable. Automatic background cutouts still need real-device validation.

    Built · device checks remain
  9. ✓

    October 21, 2026Finding what you mean

    Search and Saved Looks, refined

    Pieces can have other names and clothing types to make search more useful. Saved Looks now has a Saved / All switch, and possible matches can help you remember a name for next time.

    Built and tested
  10. ✓

    October 21, 2026Preparing the next layer

    Service safeguards and weather controls

    The account and service foundation now has tested request limits, usage allowances, and retry protection using simulated services. Weather controls support a city or optional approximate location; live weather remains off until its Apple service is enabled.

    Foundation built
  11. ✓

    October 21, 2026More control, fewer interruptions

    Privacy choices and offline handling

    The development build now includes an account-deletion flow tested with simulated services, plus better handling of interrupted requests, cancellation, and offline states. Real account connections still need activation and validation.

    Built against simulated services
  12. ✓

    October 21–22, 2026More ways to find a look

    Quick ideas and the styling connection

    Quick ideas can now make outfit suggestions on the phone without sending a request. The cloud-styling connection and background-job flow are built against simulated services, including resuming interrupted work; live styling still needs service activation.

    Built in development
  13. ✓

    October 22, 2026A little guidance before try-on

    A guided photo, with a check before saving

    The try-on camera flow now offers framing hints and a countdown, followed by a review before keeping the photo. Simulator checks use a drawn stand-in. Real-camera and on-device vision checks remain on the testing list.

    Built in development
  14. ✓

    October 22, 2026Making the process clearer

    A walkthrough of what gets sent

    The try-on flow now explains its privacy steps: cover the head and hair, replace the room, send the prepared picture, and bring the original head back on the phone. The implementation is built, with real-device and live-service validation still pending.

    Built in development
  15. ✓

    October 22, 2026An account when it is needed

    Sign-in and a way to report a result

    The development app asks for sign-in when a cloud feature needs it, rather than at launch. A result-reporting sheet is also built for cloud outfit and try-on results. These flows are tested with simulated accounts and services; real account connections are not active.

    Built in development
  16. ✓

    October 22, 2026Checking the bigger picture

    Larger wardrobes and accessibility checks

    The development build has been checked with larger sample wardrobes, and backup export now uses less memory. Accessibility checks cover the newer screens too. Simulator results guide the next round of hands-on device testing.

    Built in development
  17. ✓

    October 22, 2026Preparing optional sync

    An iCloud foundation, kept off

    Sync logic has been built and tested with two simulated devices, including edits, conflicts, account changes, and offline recovery. It remains switched off and hidden until Apple setup and real-account testing are complete; iCloud sync is not available yet.

    Built in development

  18. October 22, 2026Current stage

    Connecting, checking, and preparing

    Local wardrobe, styling fallback, guided photo setup, and service flows are built. The focus is now activation of the real services, device checks, and hands-on feedback before anyone can download the app.

    Cloud AI styling, live try-on, and iCloud sync are not active. The app is not available to download; these are development milestones.

    In development
